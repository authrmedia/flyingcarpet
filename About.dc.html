Flying Carpet & Furnace Cleaning has been an independent, owner-operated Lower Mainland business since 1989.

Our story

Not a franchise. Never has been.

Flying Carpet started cleaning Lower Mainland homes in 1989, back when most of our competitors' brands didn't exist here yet.

It has stayed hands-on and owner-operated ever since. The person who answers the phone, quotes the job, and cleans your carpet is accountable to you, not to a head office in another province.

We invested in truck-mounted hot water extraction equipment because it's simply the best tool for the job. More heat, more suction, and faster drying than any portable or rental unit. And we use eco-friendly products because we work in homes with kids, pets, and people who have to breathe the air after we leave.

Every job ends the same way: with our 24-hour satisfaction guarantee. If something isn't right, tell us within a day and we come back to make it right.
